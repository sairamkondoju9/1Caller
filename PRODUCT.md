## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, jewellery showroom managers, clinic owners, automobile dealerships, and real estate consultants in India who need autonomous multilingual AI employees to instantly call leads, answer inbound phone enquiries, handle objections, and automate WhatsApp brochure delivery.

## Product Purpose

Autonomous Voice AI Employee platform (Outpero-level UX) that drafts, configures, and deploys hyper-realistic multilingual voice agents (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, and English) in under 3 minutes. Success means sub-420ms voice response latency, zero layout breaks across all viewports, zero crash bugs, and seamless WhatsApp catalog automation upon call closing.

## Positioning

The only Indian Autonomous Voice platform offering true sub-420ms real-time voice latency via low-latency streaming pipeline (Sarvam Bulbul V4 & Cartesia Sonic-2 + Saaras ASR + Groq/Gemini/Mistral multi-provider fallback) paired with instant WhatsApp brochure & catalog delivery.

## Operating Context

- **Desktop & Mobile Responsive**: Dashboard, employee creation stepper, phone call simulator, live audio stream visualizer, analytics, instant leads calling policies, and training RAG knowledge base.
- **Languages**: Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, and global/Indian English.
- **Telephony & Messaging**: Inbound and outbound phone calls, virtual Indian telephony numbers (₹499/mo), Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API v19.0 with instant sub-200ms sandbox delivery.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **No generic AI slop**: Precise obsidian dark luxury dashboard with gold and royal violet accents matching Outpero specifications.
- **Fail-safe Error Boundaries**: Defensive null checks on every data array, object, and string transform.
- **Multi-LLM Fast Provider Cascade**: Primary Groq (sub-200ms) with automated fallback to Gemini 1.5 Flash and Mistral Large.
- **100% Real Metrics**: Real call records, live latency waterfall diagnostics, real token tracking.
- **Isolated Admin Access**: Customer workspace is clean and customer-focused; admin diagnostics reside strictly under the secret route `/?panel=admin-terminal-9482`.

## Brand Commitments

- **Ambient Bed**: Realistic call-center, office, retail, cafe, and quiet room ambient noise bed.

## Product Principles

1. **Sub-420ms Voice-to-Voice Latency**: Streaming ASR, speculative LLM tokens, and chunked TTS take precedence.
2. **Zero Layout Regressions**: Fully responsive from mobile (360px) to ultra-wide (4K).

4. **Immediate WhatsApp Closure**: When callers agree to next steps, catalog delivery executes within 200ms.
